# Comprehensive Wellbeing Framework

## 1. ESSENTIAL NEEDS

### Food
- **Tier 1:** I have enough food to eat today.
- **Tier 2:** I have food that gives me energy for the whole day.
- **Tier 3:** I enjoy most of my meals.
- **Tier 4:** I can make food choices based on what my body needs.

### Water
- **Tier 1:** I can get clean water when I need it.
- **Tier 2:** I can drink clean water at home.
- **Tier 3:** I drink water throughout the day without thinking about it.
- **Tier 4:** Staying hydrated is just part of my normal day.

### Rest
- **Tier 1:** I have a safe place to sleep.
- **Tier 2:** I wake up feeling rested most days.
- **Tier 3:** I have a bedtime routine.
- **Tier 4:** Sleep gives me energy for everything I want to do.

### Movement
- **Tier 1:** I can move around in a way that works for my body.
- **Tier 2:** I can move without pain most days.
- **Tier 3:** I look forward to moving my body.
- **Tier 4:** Moving my body makes me feel strong.

### Shelter
- **Tier 1:** I have a place that keeps me dry and safe.
- **Tier 2:** My home feels safe from dangers inside and outside.
- **Tier 3:** My home is a place I want to be.
- **Tier 4:** My home supports the life I want.

### Health
- **Tier 1:** I can get help when I don't feel well.
- **Tier 2:** I can get medicine when I need it.
- **Tier 3:** I know what keeps my body feeling good.
- **Tier 4:** I am taking steps now for my future health.

## 2. SAFETY NEEDS (Security & Stability)

### Physical Safety
- **Tier 1:** I feel safe where I live.
- **Tier 2:** I can sleep without worrying about my safety.
- **Tier 3:** I have a safe place to go when I feel scared.
- **Tier 4:** I have routines that help me feel secure daily.

### Financial Security
- **Tier 1:** I have money for my basic needs this week.
- **Tier 2:** I have enough money saved for a small emergency.
- **Tier 3:** I can plan ahead with my money.
- **Tier 4:** Building savings has improved my peace of mind.

### Job Security
- **Tier 1:** I have work or income that I can count on.
- **Tier 2:** My job or income is expected to last for the next few months.
- **Tier 3:** I have skills that help me keep or find work.
- **Tier 4:** I am growing skills that make me more valuable at work.

### Health Security
- **Tier 1:** I have a way to get help if I get sick.
- **Tier 2:** I know how to get healthcare when I need it.
- **Tier 3:** I can get care before health problems worsen.
- **Tier 4:** I have a prevention plan for my health.

### Personal Security
- **Tier 1:** I am free from threats or harm from others.
- **Tier 2:** I have people I trust in my life.
- **Tier 3:** I have people who look out for me.
- **Tier 4:** Trusted relationships have improved my sense of safety.

### Environmental Safety
- **Tier 1:** The area where I live is free from dangers.
- **Tier 2:** My home is free from things that could make me sick.
- **Tier 3:** I can make my living space safer when needed.
- **Tier 4:** I have made changes to my surroundings that improved my wellbeing.

## 3. DIGITAL WELL-BEING & ACCESS

### Digital Security
- **Tier 1:** I use passwords on my digital accounts.
- **Tier 2:** I use different passwords for important accounts.
- **Tier 3:** I regularly update my passwords and security settings.
- **Tier 4:** I have a comprehensive system for digital security.

### Tech Literacy
- **Tier 1:** I can use basic features on my digital devices.
- **Tier 2:** I can install and use new apps when needed.
- **Tier 3:** I can learn new digital tools without much frustration.
- **Tier 4:** I adapt quickly to new technologies and digital changes.

### Online Identity
- **Tier 1:** I control what personal information is shared online.
- **Tier 2:** I know what comes up when someone searches my name.
- **Tier 3:** I actively manage how I appear across digital platforms.
- **Tier 4:** My online presence authentically represents my values.

### Information Hygiene
- **Tier 1:** I can identify obviously fake information online.
- **Tier 2:** I check sources before sharing information.
- **Tier 3:** I use multiple sources to verify important information.
- **Tier 4:** I have a reliable system for managing digital information.

### AI & Automation Use
- **Tier 1:** I am aware of basic AI tools like search or assistants.
- **Tier 2:** I use AI tools to help with regular tasks.
- **Tier 3:** I customize AI tools to fit my specific needs.
- **Tier 4:** AI and automation tools enhance my productivity and creativity.

### Screen-Time Balance
- **Tier 1:** I take breaks from digital devices.
- **Tier 2:** I have tech-free times in my daily routine.
- **Tier 3:** I have boundaries between work and personal screen time.
- **Tier 4:** My technology use supports my overall wellbeing.

## 4. LOVE & BELONGING (Relationships & Community)

### Family Connection
- **Tier 1:** I have contact with family members.
- **Tier 2:** I have positive interactions with my family.
- **Tier 3:** I have healthy boundaries with family members.
- **Tier 4:** My family relationships support my growth.

### Friendship
- **Tier 1:** I have at least one person I can talk to.
- **Tier 2:** I have friends I can count on for support.
- **Tier 3:** My friendships bring joy and support to my life.
- **Tier 4:** I have deep, meaningful friendships that evolve over time.

### Social Contact
- **Tier 1:** I interact with other people regularly.
- **Tier 2:** I have regular meaningful conversations with others.
- **Tier 3:** I initiate social connections that interest me.
- **Tier 4:** I maintain a social network that energizes me.

### Group Belonging
- **Tier 1:** I am part of a group or community.
- **Tier 2:** I participate actively in a group or community.
- **Tier 3:** I feel valued in my communities.
- **Tier 4:** I contribute to communities in ways that feel meaningful.

### Acceptance
- **Tier 1:** I feel accepted by at least some people in my life.
- **Tier 2:** I feel people know the real me.
- **Tier 3:** I feel comfortable being myself around others.
- **Tier 4:** I feel truly seen and appreciated for who I am.

### Communication
- **Tier 1:** I can express my basic needs to others.
- **Tier 2:** I can share my feelings with someone.
- **Tier 3:** I can have difficult conversations when needed.
- **Tier 4:** I communicate authentically with compassion.

## 5. ESTEEM NEEDS (Achievement & Confidence)

### Self-Worth
- **Tier 1:** I believe I have value as a person.
- **Tier 2:** I generally feel good about myself.
- **Tier 3:** I value myself even when I make mistakes.
- **Tier 4:** I maintain self-respect regardless of external feedback.

### Recognition
- **Tier 1:** Others sometimes acknowledge my efforts.
- **Tier 2:** People notice when I do good work.
- **Tier 3:** Others seek out my input or expertise.
- **Tier 4:** I receive acknowledgment for my contributions.

### Competence
- **Tier 1:** I can do some things well.
- **Tier 2:** I have skills that I'm confident using.
- **Tier 3:** I am building skills in areas that matter to me.
- **Tier 4:** I continuously develop mastery in areas I value.

### Independence
- **Tier 1:** I can make some decisions for myself.
- **Tier 2:** I can solve problems on my own.
- **Tier 3:** I can take charge of important parts of my life.
- **Tier 4:** I confidently direct my own path in life.

### Respect
- **Tier 1:** I am treated with basic respect by others.
- **Tier 2:** People listen to my ideas and opinions.
- **Tier 3:** Others value my unique contributions.
- **Tier 4:** I have a reputation for excellence in some area.

### Achievement
- **Tier 1:** I have completed something that made me feel good.
- **Tier 2:** I set and reach goals for myself.
- **Tier 3:** I challenge myself with meaningful goals.
- **Tier 4:** My accomplishments align with my personal values.

## 6. SELF-ACTUALIZATION (Fulfillment & Purpose)

### Meaning
- **Tier 1:** I have things in my life that matter to me.
- **Tier 2:** I find meaning in my daily activities.
- **Tier 3:** I regularly engage in activities that feel meaningful.
- **Tier 4:** My life feels deeply meaningful and fulfilling.

### Growth
- **Tier 1:** I am learning new things sometimes.
- **Tier 2:** I am actively developing myself in some way.
- **Tier 3:** I seek out challenges that help me develop.
- **Tier 4:** I embrace challenges as opportunities for growth.

### Purpose
- **Tier 1:** I have reasons to get up in the morning.
- **Tier 2:** I have goals that feel personally important.
- **Tier 3:** I have a sense of direction in my life.
- **Tier 4:** I am living in alignment with my deepest purpose.

### Potential
- **Tier 1:** I believe I can grow and change.
- **Tier 2:** I can see possibilities for my future.
- **Tier 3:** I am working toward becoming my best self.
- **Tier 4:** I am actively realizing my unique capabilities.

### Authenticity
- **Tier 1:** I can be myself at least some of the time.
- **Tier 2:** I make choices that feel true to who I am.
- **Tier 3:** I express myself honestly in most situations.
- **Tier 4:** I live genuinely, even when it's difficult.

### Values
- **Tier 1:** I know what's important to me.
- **Tier 2:** I make decisions based on what matters to me.
- **Tier 3:** My life reflects my core values.
- **Tier 4:** My life embodies my highest values and aspirations.
